## How access works

Access is approval-gated and entitlement-based, per Brinker's own portal FAQ:

1. Create an account at https://developer.brinker.com/accounts/create
2. Account is created PENDING APPROVAL by the "Brinker API Team"
3. After approval, sign in and create a developer app requesting Default Product access
4. Brinker assigns the API Product from the backend based on your entitlement
5. Brinker activates the credentials; Apigee consumer key and secret then become visible

There is no OAuth authorization server, no OpenID Connect discovery document and no
published scope reference on any Brinker host.
